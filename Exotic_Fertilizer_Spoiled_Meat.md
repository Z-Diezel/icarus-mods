Allows Spoiled Meat and a second element to produce High-Quality, Growth, Recovery, and Resistance fertilizers in the composter, and replaces Progenitive Fertilizer with new Exotic Ultra Fertilizer.

Fertilizer stats:
Planted Crop Yield: +35%,
Planted Crop Growth Speed: +35%,
Can Harvest Seeds: Yes,
Planted Crop Seed Yield: +100%,
Crop Quality Recovery: +100%,
Crop Storm Resistance: +25%,
Fertilizer Duration: +200%
